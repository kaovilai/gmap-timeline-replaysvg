Timeline replay map

Turn a Google Maps Timeline export into a city-level world map SVG for your profile README — static or animated. Everything runs in this tab: your export is never uploaded, and only city names and city centres are written into the files you download.

Timeline.json (Android), location-history.json (iPhone), a Takeout .json, or the .zip the Maps app saves. You can also drop the file here.

Download

    Commit the SVGs to your profile repo. places.json is the city-level data they were drawn from — useful to diff when your travel changes, but not required by the SVGs.

          What is in the files
          
    
            Every sample in the export is snapped to the nearest populated place
            in a bundled gazetteer. The output contains the gazetteer's city
            name, region, country, city-centre coordinates, visit counts and
            first/last year. Raw positions, addresses and place names/IDs never
            reach the files.
